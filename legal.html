Terms of service

  • AgenticCore Pakistan is a marketing and technology provider, not a property broker. We do not sell, buy or hold property or client money for property deals.
  • All prices are in Pakistani rupees, are introductory and exclude taxes where applicable.
  • Monthly fees are paid in advance. One-off work is usually paid half at the start and half on delivery.
  • Monthly packages have a minimum term, shown beside each price. Set-up fees, where a package has one, are charged once with the first month. Monthly allowances are maximums for that month and do not roll over; extra items are charged at the published price of that service.
  • Prices shown as "from" (for example a launch kit with a website, a website, or a custom AI set-up) are starting prices; we confirm the final quote in writing before work starts. Developer Partner is quoted after scoping and is never charged without a proposal you accept.
  • Where a package includes posting, it means posting to your own social channels (pages you own). Separately, eligible client properties and projects may be featured on AgenticCore's own property-marketing channels as those channels are operated and developed. This featuring is optional, depends on content suitability and platform rules, and is not guaranteed for any package. We never guarantee views, reach, enquiries or sales.
  • AI and automation set-ups run in your own accounts. Running costs (for example AI usage, WhatsApp messaging, hosting or telephony) are billed to you by those providers at their current rates and are not included in our set-up fee.
  • Each service shows its own delivery time. We work 7 days a week: every day counts towards delivery, including weekends and public holidays. Delivery times count from when we have your details, content (such as logo files, photos and text) and payment. Orders confirmed before 6pm PKT start the same day; orders confirmed at or after 6pm PKT start the following day. "Same day" work is due by 9pm PKT on its start day, and a turnaround of "2–3 days" is counted in calendar days from the start day. While a task is waiting on you, its delivery clock is paused.
  • Partner work (printing, drone filming, billboards and hoardings, on-site 360° photography, event crews) and approvals by Meta, Google or phone providers follow their own timings and are quoted per job.
  • Each design includes two rounds of changes. Further changes are quoted separately.
  • You own the finished work and may use it for your business. Ad accounts, pages, domains, WhatsApp numbers and automations are set up in your name, so you keep them.
  • We only advertise approvals (NOC, RDA, CDA, LDA, SBCA, KDA and others) that you can show us.
  • We never promise a set number of leads or sales. We agree clear targets and report honestly.
  • Advertising wording checks (service 65) are a marketing check, not legal advice.
  • Urdu agreement first drafts and the agreement drafting assistant (services 70 and 71) are for a clear, shared understanding between the parties only. They are not for final or real agreement signing until your own legal adviser has checked them, and they are not legal advice. Stamp paper, registration and transfer steps stay with you and your adviser. Please check names, CNIC numbers, property details and amounts yourself.

Privacy

  • Your AgenticCore account is shared with our sister site agenticcore.estate: the same login, profile and points balance work on both.
  • We store what you give us to deliver your orders: your name, phone, email, business details, brand kit files and the briefs and files attached to your tasks. Files are kept in private storage; only you and our team can open them.
  • When you submit a callback or free-offer form we store your name, phone, city and what you asked about, so we can contact you.
  • We record simple, cookie-free page events (for example "WhatsApp button clicked in the packages section") to improve the site. They are not linked to your name.
  • We do not sell your data. To delete your account or data, message us.

Referral terms

  • Every AgenticCore account has one referral link and code. There are no levels, tiers or membership cards.
  • When an account that signed up with your link or code (a direct referral) pays for anything on an AgenticCore site, you earn 10% of the amount paid as AgenticCore Points. 1 point = Rs 1.
  • Points are credited once an invoice is marked paid. Only direct referrals count; people referred by your referrals do not.
  • Points and your referral list are shared across agenticcore.estate and agenticcorepk.com. This is a different system from AgenticCore Agency's points, which are separate.
  • How points can be spent will be announced before redemption opens.
